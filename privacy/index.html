Privacy stays on your map.

Training Log Merge is designed to work without an account. Workout files, manual sessions, notes, labels, source links, and settings are processed in your browser and stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Local data

Your training ledger is stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. Your display time zone and, if you buy the Field Kit, your license token and cached verification result are stored in localStorage. Clearing site data deletes this information. Use CSV export or the paid JSON backup before clearing it if you want a copy.

Network requests

After first load, the installed app can work offline. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or social scripts. Your browser may request the app files from our host. Standard short-lived server logs may include an IP address and user agent for security and reliability and are not joined to your workout data.

Purchases

If you choose the one-time Field Kit, checkout and license verification use the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record, process purchase information under their own policies. The app sends the license token for verification; it does not send your workouts.

Your control

You can edit or delete individual sessions, export all sessions to CSV, create a JSON backup with the Field Kit, or remove everything by clearing this site’s storage in your browser. Because there is no Training Log Merge account or server-side training database, we cannot recover a deleted local ledger.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
